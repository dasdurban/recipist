Privacy Policy — The Recipe App

Last updated: 19 October 2026

The Recipe App ("the app") is a personal recipe organiser. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and what control you have over it.

The short version: your recipes stay on your device. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of ours that receive your data. The only time information leaves your device is when you ask it to — importing a recipe from a website, or syncing to storage you set up yourself.

Who we are

The Recipe App is developed and published by Victor ("we", "us"). For any question about this policy or your data, contact dasleveille@gmail.com.

Information the app handles

Recipes and notes you create or import

Recipes, ingredients, instructions, categories, shopping lists, event plans, photos and any notes you add are stored locally on your device in the app's own storage area. This includes recipes you type in yourself, recipes imported from a website URL you paste, and photos you import including any text extracted from them. This information is not sent to us, and we cannot see it. We operate no server that receives your recipes.

Photos

If you import a photo (for example, a photograph of a recipe card), the image is saved with the recipe on your device. Text is extracted from the image on your device to help fill in the recipe. The photo is not uploaded to us.

Settings

Your preferences — theme, colours, layout, proxy settings, sync configuration — are stored locally on your device alongside your recipes.

Sync credentials (only if you enable sync)

If you turn on sync, you choose a storage provider and enter your own credentials for it (for example, a WebDAV server address, username and password, or OAuth client details). Those credentials are stored on your device and are used only to connect to the provider you named. They are not sent to us.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, and the app does not contain:

  • any analytics or usage tracking;
  • any crash reporting or telemetry;
  • any advertising or advertising identifiers;
  • any device fingerprinting;
  • any account, email address, name or sign-in of any kind;
  • any location data;
  • any contact list, calendar or health data.

We have no way to identify you, and we do not attempt to.

When information leaves your device

There are exactly three situations, all of them started by you.

1. Importing a recipe from a website

When you paste a recipe URL and choose to import it, the app requests that page from the website in question. To make this work, the app may route the request through a CORS proxy — a service that fetches the page on your behalf. By default the app uses one of these public proxies:

  • api.allorigins.win
  • api.codetabs.com
  • corsproxy.io
  • api.cors.lol

You can replace this in Settings with your own proxy. Whatever URL you are importing is visible to whichever proxy is in use, so choose one you are comfortable with — or configure your own.

The recipe page itself is fetched from the website you named, so that website sees a request from you in the ordinary way, as any browser visit would.

2. Sync, if you turn it on

If you enable sync, your recipes are copied to the storage provider you configured — for example your own WebDAV server, or iCloud, OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox. That provider's own privacy policy governs what it does with the data. We are not involved in, and cannot see, that transfer. Sync is off by default.

3. Fetching supporting resources

Some optional features download supporting files. Text recognition (OCR) uses the Tesseract engine, whose worker files and language data are downloaded from a public content delivery network the first time you use the feature. These downloads request only the resource itself. No content of yours is sent with them.

How your information is stored and secured

Your data is stored in your device's standard application storage area, which is protected by your operating system's normal file protections and by any disk encryption you use. If you enable sync, data in transit to your provider is protected by that provider's transport security (HTTPS/TLS).

We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to secure, breach or lose on your behalf.

How long information is kept

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Deleting a recipe, category or photo removes it from local storage. Uninstalling the app removes its local data too, subject to your platform's behaviour. Because we hold nothing, there is no retention period on our side.

Your control over your information

  • Access and export — the app includes a backup feature that exports your data to a file you control.
  • Correction — edit anything directly in the app.
  • Deletion — delete individual recipes, photos or categories, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
  • Withdrawal of sync — turn sync off at any time; a copy may remain with the provider you configured until you delete it there.

Requests about data we hold are not meaningful, because we hold none. If you think we do, contact us at the address above.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
